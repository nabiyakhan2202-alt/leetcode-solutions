# Two Sum

**Difficulty:** Easy

**LeetCode:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach

I used two nested loops to check every possible pair of elements. If the sum of two elements is equal to the target, their indices are returned.

## Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

## Notes

I learned that the indices of the two numbers are required as the answer. I also tested the solution locally before submitting it to LeetCode.
